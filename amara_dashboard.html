📐 How the Score Works ▼

Each stock is scored out of 100 points across 4 momentum criteria. A stock needs 60+ points to pass and be sent to Claude for final approval.

Criterion Points What it means
RSI between 55–75 30 pts RSI measures buying momentum (0–100). Below 55 = not yet moving. Above 75 = overbought, risky to buy. The 55–75 zone is the "momentum sweet spot."
Price > 20-day avg 25 pts Stock is trading above its 20-day moving average — an uptrend confirmation. Stocks below their average are still in decline or sideways.
5-day avg > 20-day avg 20 pts Short-term average crossing above long-term average (mini "golden cross") — signals that recent momentum is accelerating.
Volume ≥ 1.5× average 25 pts Today's volume is 1.5× the 20-day average — a surge means institutions or big money are moving in, not just retail noise.

After scoring, the top 5 stocks are sent to Claude for a second opinion on risk/reward. Claude needs a confidence score of 6/10 or higher to approve a buy.
